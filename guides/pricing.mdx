Before you send, you can get a live rate and fee for any corridor. It's a quote for display — no money moves, nothing is saved.

## Get a rate

```bash
GET /v1/fx/convert
```

Pass the amount, the currencies, and the direction:

```bash
curl "https://api.stablepay.ai/v1/fx/convert?amount=1000&sourceCurrency=KES&destinationCurrency=USDC&direction=payout&paymentMethod=MOMO&optionalIssuerFee=NO" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer spay_live_xxxx"
```

```json
{
  "convertedAmount": "7.6",
  "exchangeRate": "128.700000",
  "paymentFee": "10",
  "expiryTimestamp": 1783330000
}
```

- `amount` is always the local fiat amount.
- `convertedAmount` is the stablecoin you spend (payout) or receive (pay-in).
- `paymentFee` is the fee, in the local currency.

## How the rate is built

We take the live provider rate and apply your margin:

| Direction | Standard (`NO`) | With issuer fee (`YES`) |
|---|---|---|
| Payout | rate × 0.99 | rate × 0.985 |
| Pay-in | rate × 1.01 | rate × 1.015 |

Set `optionalIssuerFee=YES` to add the extra 0.5% (1.5% total) or `NO` for the standard 1%.

<Note>The rate is indicative. The final rate is taken when the payment is created, so a quote and a payment made moments apart can differ slightly.</Note>
