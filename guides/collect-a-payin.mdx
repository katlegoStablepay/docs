---
title: Collect a pay-in
description: "Take local fiat from a customer and receive stablecoin"
---

A pay-in is the reverse of a payout. A customer pays local currency — usually with a mobile-money prompt on their phone — and you receive stablecoin.

## The request

```bash
POST /v1/payments/in
```

As with payouts, send a unique `Idempotency-Key` header.

```bash
curl https://api.stablepay.ai/v1/payments/in \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer spay_live_xxxx" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: topup-001" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "fiatAmount": "1000",
    "sourceCurrency": "KES",
    "destinationCurrency": "USDC",
    "blockchain": "BSC",
    "paymentMethod": "MOMO",
    "accountName": "Alice Wanjiku",
    "accountNumber": "+254712345678",
    "paymentCode": "MPESA",
    "reference": "topup-001"
  }'
```

Here `sourceCurrency` is the local currency the customer pays, and `destinationCurrency` is the stablecoin you receive.

## How it settles

1. The customer gets a prompt to approve the payment on their phone.
2. Once they pay, the stablecoin is credited to you.
3. The payment reaches `succeeded`.

Track it the same way as a payout — poll [`GET /v1/payments/:id`](/get-payment) or listen for a [webhook](/guides/webhooks).

## Bank pay-ins: deposit instructions

Some corridors collect fiat by **bank transfer** rather than a mobile-money prompt. For those, the pay-in response includes a `depositInstruction` object with the bank account the customer must transfer into:

```json
{
  "paymentId": "pay_01HV8KZMRJPQR9S7T3XABCDEF",
  "status": "provider_accepted",
  "convertedAmount": "3.00",
  "depositInstruction": {
    "type": "fiat",
    "bankName": "Paystack-Titan",
    "bankCode": "629",
    "accountName": "BDL CHECKOUT",
    "accountNumber": "9706072196",
    "expiresAt": "2026-07-15T15:14:01Z"
  }
}
```

Surface these details to your customer so they can complete the transfer. The account is **time-limited** — if `expiresAt` passes before they pay, start a new pay-in. `depositInstruction` is also returned on [`GET /v1/payments/:id`](/get-payment) while the deposit is pending, and is absent for mobile-money (MOMO) pay-ins, which push a prompt to the customer instead.
