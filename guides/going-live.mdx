Before you switch on real traffic, run through this.

## Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use a live key">
    Your production key starts with `spay_live_`. Keep it on your server — never in a browser or mobile app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send an idempotency key">
    Add a unique `Idempotency-Key` to every payment. It's what makes a retry safe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle the failure states">
    Payments can end at `failed`, and creation can return `502` when a provider is briefly down. Make sure your code handles both, not just the happy path.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your webhooks">
    Check the signature on every event before acting on it, and make your handler safe to run twice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry sensibly">
    On `5xx`, retry with backoff. On `4xx`, fix the request — retrying won't help.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Confirm your corridors

Each corridor is switched on for your account before you can use it. If a payment returns `422 wallet_not_enabled`, that corridor isn't live for you yet — [contact us](mailto:hello@stablepay.io) and we'll enable it.
